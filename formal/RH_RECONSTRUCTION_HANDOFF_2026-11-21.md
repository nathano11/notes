## Matrix handoff

In the conductor-69 paper model, Y is the 2-adic lattice on (b,c) and X is its index-two sublattice on (2b,c). For a positive odd weight r and layer a, write D_a = γ^(2^a) − (5^(-r))^(2^a) I. The predicted arithmetic dual is X/D_aY, not Y/D_aY: the two local vanishing conditions account for the index-two distinction. The order in this model is 2^(2a+1), giving a contribution (2a+1) log 2 at a fixed such weight if the arithmetic identification holds.

[DyadicMatrixHandoff.lattice_invariant_factors](Reimann/DyadicMatrixHandoff.lean) supplies inverse read/write maps, explicit residue coordinates and preservation of addition under WeightOneSamples. [DyadicStrictDualAdapter.strict_dual_invariant_factors](Reimann/DyadicStrictDualAdapter.lean) transports the result to a group with the specified additive surjection and exact kernel (Presentation). The lattice theorem accepts natural r,a; the actual strict-group application requires positive odd weight. The formal signatures keep the actual Iwasawa coefficient samples and Artin/reciprocity presentation explicit. Consult [the proof status](PROOF_STATUS.md) for the field-specific construction checkpoints rather than treating the matrix calculation as an unconditional field theorem.

This is one part of the dyadic contribution to the RH window integer, not the complementary odd-primary bound, not a proof for all varying (q,d), and not the same matrix issue as the ordinary RH lattice-slope estimate. See [the matrix manuscript](../Strict_Dyadic_Invariant_Factors.md).
